About Me

Hey, I'm Gev!
Nice to meet you :)

I'm a UX Designer who has led and collaborated with teams of designers, product managers, and developers to bring ideas to life.

My passion for design ignited in the mid-2000s when my brother gifted me Photoshop. 13-year-old me was blown away by the capabilities of the software. I've been hooked to creative outlets ever since. Over the years, I've honed my skills across various design disciplines including Photography, Motion Graphics, UX/UI, Branding, and Web; enriching my expertise and pushing creative boundaries.

I even know how to trim and shape my own beard, that's design oriented too, right?

Outside of design some of my hobbies include playing guitar and drums, longboarding, playing football, losing in fantasy football, and of course, BAKING! I also enjoy playing video games and reading, one of which I definitely do more than the other...
